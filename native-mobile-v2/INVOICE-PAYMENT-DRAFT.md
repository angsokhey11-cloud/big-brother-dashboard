# Online draft payment calculations
Uses fixed or percentage discount, USD/KHR displayed precision, explicit daily rate, cash/bank/credit/partial/split payment previews and separate actual bank currency. BigDecimal is used instead of binary floating point. Amounts in this draft are rounded at invoice currency precision; compare rounding with authoritative existing payload/SQL before enabling Complete. Partial payment validation is stricter than the old preview's clamping. Bank equivalent is informational only; registered balances, approved cross-rates and source selection are not yet connected. No payment registration, invoice save, or stock writes. Complete remains disabled. Local and production remain untouched.

# Online Invoice Generator migration
Native draft screen reads live bootstrap, open batches, scoped customers, suggested sequence and customer prices through existing authenticated RPCs. The existing route.invoice canCreate permission is required. No invoice, stock, payment or sequence writes. Draft is in memory only. This is not an operational sales workflow.
Before enabling Complete: migrate exact purchased/zero-cost stock and warehouse source rules, product/group selling units, stock availability, customer mismatch confirmation, discount, credit term and USD/KHR exchange handling, all payment modes and bank allocations, duplicate invoice/customer guard, atomic bundle and uncertain-save recovery. Verify batch selling dates again at submission; preserve stable invoice ID for retries. Test backend transactional and permission enforcement and compare against current system. Existing production and Local V2 remain unchanged.
